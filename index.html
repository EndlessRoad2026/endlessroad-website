Three worlds

Every world plays differently

The road never ends, and it's different every time you play. Each world has its own weather, dangers and things to find.

🏜️ Desert

Long hot roads, dunes and towering red rocks. Push too hard and your engine overheats. When a sandstorm rolls in you can barely see the road.

Watch the engine heat

🏔️ Snowy Mountains

Icy roads, steep climbs and freezing cold. Keep the heater running, but it burns fuel. Blizzards hide the road and snowdrifts block your way.

Stay warm

🌴 Jungle

Muddy tracks, fallen logs and thick trees. When the rain comes the mud gets deeper and the road gets slippery. Grab bananas and coconuts on the way.

Mind the mud
What you can do

Drive, explore, survive

🚗

Three vehicles

A pickup with a load bed, a sedan and an off-road buggy with a roll cage.

🔧

Take cars apart

Open the bonnet and doors. Take off wheels, doors, the battery or radiator, and swap in parts from wrecks.

🏚️

Scavenge

Abandoned houses hide food, jerry cans, tool kits, spare wheels and CDs.

🍞

Stay alive

Keep an eye on your health, stamina and hunger. In the snow, warmth matters too.

📻

Car radio

Four radio stations plus a CD player. Every song is original music made for the game.

🧰

Sandbox mode

Spawn cars and items, switch hunger off, change the weather and just play around.

Behind the wheel

Switch to first-person and drive from the driver's seat, with working dials, warning lights and the radio in the dash.

Or press V to see the car from behind. Your choice.

Fix it yourself

Broke down in the middle of nowhere? Get out, pop the bonnet and see what's wrong. Missing a wheel? There's a rusty wreck just off the road…

Every part you can see, you can take off and put back on.

Updates

What's new

Endless Road keeps growing. Here's everything that's been added so far.

4 November 2026

Version 4 latest

  • Your game saves by itself. Pick Continue to carry on, or start a new game
  • Play with friends: share a 5-letter code and they join your world
  • Take out the engine and seats, and pick from four engines (Inline-4, V6, V8, Diesel) and three seat types
  • Tyres come off rims, with four tyre types and four rim styles
  • Radiators now fit every vehicle, and parts show up properly when you put them back
  • Crouch with Shift (run is now Q)
  • Chickens that wander, peck and lay eggs. You can pick them up!
  • Sandbox guns for shooting targets and knocking things over (chickens just flap away)
  • More detailed items in the sandbox menu
4 November 2026

Version 3

  • First-person view (press V) with mouse look and a crosshair
  • A proper dashboard with working dials, warning lights and a radio screen
  • Bonnets, doors, boots and tailgates open on hinges. Hold E to take them off
  • Detailed engine bay, plus a removable battery and radiator
  • New vehicles: the pickup and the buggy
  • Sandbox mode with a spawn menu for vehicles and items
  • Hotbar, stamina, and CDs to collect for the car radio
  • Power lines, rock towers, clouds and better houses with doors that open
